Privacy policy

Lunta for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and Linux · last updated 16 November 2026

In short

Lunta collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Your library, highlights and notes are yours and stay in your own storage.

Where your library is kept

  • On iPhone, iPad and Mac, in your own iCloud Drive. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; the developer of Lunta has no access to it.
  • On Android, Windows and Linux (and on a Mac that bridges to them), in a “Lunta” folder in your own Google Drive, if you choose to connect it. Lunta uses that access only to keep the “Lunta” folder in sync, and the sign-in tokens stay on your device.

What Lunta sends over the internet

To find papers and their details, Lunta looks them up in public scholarly services, sending only what it is looking up (a DOI, a title, search words, the topics you follow in New for You): OpenAlex, Crossref, Europe PMC and PubMed Central, PubMed, arXiv, bioRxiv/medRxiv, eLife, and doi.org. To download open-access PDFs and figures it contacts the publisher or archive that hosts them. These requests carry no personal information, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to that service.

  • Ask (the assistant) runs on your device: with Apple Intelligence, or with a free open model that Lunta downloads once (from Hugging Face) and then runs offline. Your questions and papers are not sent to any server.
  • Natural voices for listening are downloaded once and then run on your device.
  • The browser extension and the Word and Google Docs add-ins talk only to the Lunta app on your own computer.

On your device

Lunta stores its settings, a search index and caches on your device. Notifications (the morning New for You notice) are scheduled on the device. Deleting the app removes them; your library stays in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete it there.

Children

Lunta is a research tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date. Questions: GitHub Issues.
