Lunta Citations for Word

Cite papers from your Lunta library and build the reference list in APA, Vancouver or Nature style.

What you need

The Lunta app, open on the same computer as Word. The add-in reads your library from the app; it has no library of its own.

Setup (once)

  1. In Lunta, choose Lunta › Citations in Word & Google Docs… and click Trust Certificate. This lets Word talk to Lunta securely on your computer; your Mac asks for your password once.
  2. In Word: Home › Add-ins, search for Lunta Citations and add it.

Using it

  • Click Cite on the Home tab. Search your library, pick one or more papers, then Insert Citation.
  • Put the cursor where the references go and click Insert Bibliography Here.
  • After editing, or after changing the style, click Update All: citations renumber and the reference list is rebuilt.

Troubleshooting

  • “Can’t reach Lunta”: open the Lunta app. If it is open, do setup step 1 again.
  • Citations look out of date: click Update All.
