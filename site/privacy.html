Privacy policy

Lunta Citations add-in for Microsoft Word · last updated 13 November 2026

In short

The add-in collects nothing. Your library and your documents stay on your computer.

How it works

  • The add-in’s page is loaded from this website (GitHub Pages). It contains no analytics, no trackers and no cookies.
  • When you search or cite, the page talks only to the Lunta app on your own computer, at https://localhost. Nothing you search for and no paper details are sent to any server.
  • Citations and the reference list are written into your Word document by Word itself.
  • The add-in stores one setting in the document: the citation style you chose.

Third parties

Loading the page from GitHub Pages means GitHub receives the usual web request information (such as your IP address), as for any website; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Word loads Microsoft’s Office JavaScript library from Microsoft; see Microsoft’s privacy statement.
