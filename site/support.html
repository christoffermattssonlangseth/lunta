Lunta Citations for Word

Cite papers from your Lunta library and build the reference list in APA, Vancouver, Nature and nine more styles, or any of about 10,000 journal styles.

What you need

The Lunta app, open on the same computer as Word, to search and cite. The add-in reads your library from the app; it has no library of its own. Citations already in a document stay as they are without Lunta, and the document keeps a copy of the details of the papers it cites, so Update All still renumbers and rebuilds the reference list when Lunta is closed, on another computer, or for a paper since removed from your library.

Setup (once)

  1. In Lunta, choose Lunta › Citations in Word & Google Docs… and click Trust Certificate. This lets Word talk to Lunta securely on your computer; your Mac asks for your password once.
  2. In Word: Home › Add-ins, search for Lunta Citations and add it.

Using it

  • Click Cite on the Home tab. Search your library, pick one or more papers, then Insert Citation.
  • Put the cursor where the references go and click Insert Bibliography Here.
  • After editing, or after changing the style, click Update All: citations renumber and the reference list is rebuilt. Running it again changes nothing. The style is saved in the document.
  • To add papers to a citation already in the text, click inside it, pick the papers and click Insert Citation. To remove a citation, delete its text.
  • Styles: APA, Harvard, Chicago, Cell, MLA, Vancouver, IEEE, AMA, Nature, Science, PNAS and ACS are built in. For a specific journal, choose Find journal style… in the style menu and search its name: about 10,000 journal styles from the Citation Style Language catalogue.

Troubleshooting

  • “Can’t reach Lunta”: open the Lunta app. If it is open, do setup step 1 again: the certificate is renewed by Lunta every two years without asking, but if Lunta had to make a new one, it must be trusted again (the Citations window shows step 1 as not done).
  • Citations look out of date: click Update All.
  • “Cited papers aren’t in your Lunta library”: the selected citation cites a paper Lunta doesn’t have and the document has no copy of (for example a document from before this was kept, written with another library). Nothing was changed. Add the paper to Lunta, or delete the citation and cite it again.
  • “This document’s citation style wasn’t saved with it”: the document was made with another copy of the add-in. Choose its style in the menu; the add-in won’t rewrite citations until you do.
  • A citation in the reference list you deleted from the text: with Track Changes on, a deleted citation counts until the deletion is accepted.
